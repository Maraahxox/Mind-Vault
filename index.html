WHAT IS MIND VAULT?

What is Mind Vault all about?

Mind Vault is a decentralized protocol that allows anyone to create an AI-powered digital identity, this is an intelligent version of you that evolves with your data, personality, and experiences.
It is secured by a Soulbound NFT, stored on-chain, and designed to follow you across apps, platforms, and digital worlds.

Your Mind Vault becomes your personal intelligence layer, your memory vault, and your identity engine.

WHY MIND VAULT?

Why Mind Vault Matters

  1. Identity belongs to people, not corporations.
  2. Current AI systems don't remember you.
  3. Web2 profiles don't evolve.
  4. Digital identities are fragmented across platforms.


Mind Vault fixes all of this with one protocol powered by:

✨ AI ✨ Identity ✨ Soulbound NFTs ✨ Persistent Evolution


We call these four pillars "The Triangle"

Hence, no one has combined these four pillars before.
That's why we are the world's first.

HOW IT WORKS

How Mind Vault Works

  1. Create Your Vault
    You mint a Soulbound NFT that represents your identity on-chain.
  2. Train Your Mind Vault
    Your AI evolves as you interact. This is done by learning your style, tone, preferences, and behavior.
  3. Own Your Data
    Your identity isn't stored on any central server. You control access and permissions.
  4. Use It Anywhere
    Apps, games, and digital worlds can integrate Mind Vault to allow login, personalization, and AI-powered features.
